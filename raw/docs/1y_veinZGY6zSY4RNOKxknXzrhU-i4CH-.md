[MPF Original](https://www.fhlbtopeka.com/Portals/0/Documents/prod-mpf/mpfproducts/MPF_CE_Upfront.pdf?ver=2017-06-05-212014-207) - Credit Enhancement

Similar to the mpf program’s traditional risk-sharing products, the mpf original – credit enhancement (ce) upfront option shares the risks associated with home mortgage finance between FHLBank Topeka and its members to maximize the value of mortgages being sold into the secondary market. Participating Financial Institutions (PFIs) receive additional compensation for providing a credit enhancement obligation that supports master commitment pools of sold loans. The loan pools are further supported by an FHLBank spread account (referred to as the first loss account) and any loan losses exceeding the PFI-provided CE Obligation are absorbed by FHLBank.

The MPF Original – CE Upfront option differs from other risk-sharing products in that it allows the member to take the compensation, the CE fee, for providing the credit enhancement in the form of an upfront price adjustment instead of receiving fees over the life of the loans. Both options provide a superior execution for managing that risk. However, the CE Upfront option may be preferable for participating members that:

  - Typically view the CE fee as ancillary income after the sale decision is made and the transaction has been completed. By monetizing the CE fee into the asset price, PFIs will realize additional income in their secondary market operations, where the loan sale decision is made.   

Do not want to build special processes. Because the CE fee income is unique in the industry, PFIs need to build special processes to recognize the value when determining the execution. With the MPF Original - CE Upfront option, additional processes are not needed to account for the CE fee or assign value at the institution or departmental level. The member is paid an enhanced upfront price in place of a CE fee income annuity over time.

[MPF Government Mortgage-backed Securities (MBS)](http://marketing.fhlbtopeka.com/acton/attachment/3202/f-04bc/1/-/-/-/-/FHLBNational_MPFGovernmentMBS.pdf)

MPF Government MBS is an MPF Program government loan product whereby the Federal Home Loan Bank of Chicago purchases government loans from eligible Participating Financial Institutions (PFIs). The purchased loans will be aggregated and pooled into securities guaranteed by the Government National Mortgage Association (Ginnie Mae). Loans that qualify under this product are fixed-rate mortgage loans insured or guaranteed by the following government agencies:

FHA - Low down payment options

VA - Financing and down payment flexibility for military veterans

RHS Section 502 - Flexible financing for borrowers in rural and agricultural areas

If choosing the servicing released option, you would sell the servicing to an approved MPF servicing aggregator and receive a competitive servicing released premium. Competitive pricing and servicing options distinguish the MPF Government MBS product as a viable alternative to other secondary market outlets.

The MPF Government MBS Product Benefits PFIs That:   
  
• Are looking for a competitively priced government loan investor   
  
• Are approved by the applicable government agency to originate and service loans

MPF Xtra offers Participating Financial Institutions (PFIs) the ability to leverage their membership in their Federal Home Loan Bank and access its low-cost options to sell fixed-rate, conforming loans through its partnership arrangement with Fannie Mae. PFIs that take advantage of the MPF Xtra product have the flexibility to:

• Offer fixed-rate residential mortgage loans to their borrowers and deliver those loans into the secondary market

• Retain the servicing rights, earn servicing fee income, and preserve the ability to cultivate and maintain relationships with customers

• Sell the servicing rights and receive a servicing-released premium And, since the PFI does not retain credit risk for loans sold under the MPF Xtra product, there are no collateral or risk-based capital requirements.\*
