**\*\*Dental Coverage Under Medicare**

Medicare is a federal health insurance program for people 65 and older and for people under 65 with specific disabilities. Medicare only covers dental services related to certain medical conditions or treatments. It does not cover dentures or most routine care like checkups, cleanings, or fillings. Visit [Medicare Dental Services](http://www.medicare.gov/coverage/dental-services.html) or call 1–800–MEDICARE (1–800–633–4227). Have your Medicare number handy when you call.

[**https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/dental-services.html**](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/dental-services.html)

 

  
  

**\*\*\*Dental Care for Children Up to 19 Years**

CHIP is a state-run program for children whose families earn too much to qualify for Medicaid but can't afford private insurance. CHIP provides dental services to children up to age 19. Dental services covered under this program vary from state to state. To find children's dental care programs in your state, visit [Insure Kids Now](https://www.insurekidsnow.gov/) or call 1–877–KIDS–NOW (1–877–543–7669)

**\*\*\*Dental Coverage Under Medicaid**

Medicaid is a state-run program that provides medical benefits — and in some cases dental benefits — to eligible individuals and families. States are required to provide dental benefits for children covered by Medicaid, but states can choose whether to provide dental benefits for adults. Most states provide only limited dental services for adults, while some offer extensive services. Visit the [Medicaid](http://www.medicaid.gov/) website and click on "Learn How to Apply for Coverage" or contact your [state Medicaid program](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid-chip-program-information/by-state/by-state.html).
