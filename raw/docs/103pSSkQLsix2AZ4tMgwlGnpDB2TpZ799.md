\*\*\*Don’t Pay for Health or Dental Care When You Have No Insurance

Intro Video:

<https://youtu.be/aDsvazToTjg>

The US Government Human Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) maintains a database of health centers that care for you, even if you have no health insurance – you pay what you can afford based on your income. Health

centers provide services that include checkups when you are well, treatment when you are sick, complete care when you are pregnant, and immunizations and checkups for your children. Some health centers also provide mental

health, substance abuse, oral health, and/or vision services. Contact the health center organization directly to confirm the availability of specific services and to make an appointment.

<https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov/>
