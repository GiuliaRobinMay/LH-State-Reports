Q: Why does your state want to help you start a business, become self-employed or create an organization?

A: Because of jobs. If you create a job for yourself or for others you will pay more taxes, be satisfied in life and re-elect the current politicians. That is why the state has so many programs to help you.

The best-kept secret for researching for money for your venture... don’t ask for a grant\! In the beginning of any research, it is best to say… “I need help to start (or grow) my…..” This way you are open to learning about dozens of other ways to get money and help for your venture, other than the form of grants.
