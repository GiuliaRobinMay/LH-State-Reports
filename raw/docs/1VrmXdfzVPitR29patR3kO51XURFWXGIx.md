Q. Why does Arkansas want to help you start a business, become self-employed, or create an organization?

A. Because of jobs. If you create a job for yourself or for others, you will pay more taxes, be satisfied in life, and re-elect the current politicians. That is why the state has so many programs to help you.

The sources below are just the starting places and a few samples. The real work to take these sources and work with them is up to you. Ask questions about other sources. And then do the same with those sources. It’s not easy. You must take your time. It is like panning for gold or solving a crime on CSI.

These are the best-kept secrets for researching for money for your venture.

“Don’t Ask For A Grant”

In the beginning of any research, it is best to say… “I Need Help to Start (or grow) My…..”

This way, you are open to learning about dozens of other ways to get money and help for your venture that are not called grants.
